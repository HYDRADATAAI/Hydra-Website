WHAT IT DOES

Market evidence becomes governed, traceable objects.

HYDRA is structured around ingestion, source-symbol normalization and deterministic event identity, explicit handoff contracts, provenance/history, and auditable downstream interpretation.

DATA FLOW
SOURCE EVIDENCE ↓ NORMALIZATION ↓ SOURCE-SYMBOL NORMALIZATION + DETERMINISTIC EVENT IDENTITY ↓ CONTRACT / AUTHORITY GATE ↓ PROVENANCE + HISTORY ↓ CONSTRAINT INTERPRETATION ↓ AUDITABLE RESULT
ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Correctness is part of the pipeline.

Explicit authority, deterministic identity, provenance, contract-bound handoffs, testable transformations, preserved lineage, and blocked rather than fabricated outputs.

SHADOW CONTROL PRINCIPLE

Discoverable data is not automatically authorized data.

HYDRA treats field ownership as a contract question. A plausible value elsewhere in the project does not become admissible merely because it can be found.

ADMITrequired field + proven owner or permitted derivation
BLOCKmissing authority → stop → invent nothing

REAL VS SYNTHETIC

The labels are part of the design.

REPRESENTATIVE: the market constraint walkthrough and illustrative source/normalization/result objects. SYNTHETIC / NON-LIVE: the Python pipeline and checkpoint-recovery fixtures, SQLite quality fixture, separately committed qrels, independent of the behavior cases, committed structured-grounding candidate fixtures, and deterministic proof package shown publicly. The bundle includes source_snapshot.csv and resolved_symbol_aliases.json; all seven synthetic rows, including quarantine-designed rows, are included. Governed contexts and receipts remain accepted-only or aggregate-only and expose no quarantined row payloads. The governed sample emits explicit ADMIT, QUARANTINE, ABSTAIN, and REFUSE outcomes; its candidate responses are fixtures, not model output. This evidence covers deterministic input-to-artifact replay, lexical retrieval, and structured grounding; no semantic or embedding retrieval, model, agent, broker, or external action is executed. SYNTHETIC / SHADOW: the integration receipts where labeled. NOT CLAIMED: live production operation, production SLO attainment, deployed orchestration, a production database or warehouse, proven real-world constraint detection, production promotion, model quality, semantic or embedding retrieval, production inference, agent execution, independent attestation, or any executed model or external action in the governed sample.
