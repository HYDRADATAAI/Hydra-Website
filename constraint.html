01 / CLASSIFICATION

Friction is a signal.
Constraint is a claim.

HYDRA keeps those ideas separate. A classification should describe the market mechanism without pretending every inconvenience, price move, or capacity issue is the same thing.

CLASS 01MARKET MECHANISM

Actual constraint

A binding limit materially restricts feasible throughput, access, capacity, or action.

CORE QUESTIONWhat is genuinely preventing more of the desired outcome?
EVIDENCE TO KEEPscope · time window · causal mechanism · competing evidence
COMMON FAILUREcalling correlated friction a causal constraint
DOWNSTREAM VALUEprioritized, explainable market constraint case
DISCIPLINE

A constraint claim should be narrower than the evidence, never broader.

02 / DIMENSIONS

A type is only the beginning.

The same constraint can behave very differently depending on scope, duration, severity, reversibility, beneficiaries, and risk. HYDRA keeps those dimensions explicit instead of burying them in prose.

A

Hard / Soft

Is the limit physically or structurally binding, or can behavior and process work around it?

B

Local / Systemic

Does the effect bind one entity, region, or stage, or propagate across the market system?

C

Current / Emerging

Is it binding now, or is the evidence describing a constraint that is forming?

D

Severity

How strongly does the limit affect feasible throughput, access, price, or execution?

E

Reversibility

Can the constraint be relieved quickly, or does it require structural change?

F

Beneficiary + Risk

Who benefits, who absorbs the burden, and what risk is created if the constraint persists?

03 / EVIDENCE MODEL

Preserve disagreement instead of voting it away.

A constraint case can contain supporting evidence, limiting evidence, reversals, stale observations, and duplicate or overlapping sources. HYDRA keeps that history available for adjudication.

SUPPORTEvidence that the limit is real or strengthening.source identity · timestamp · case scope · claim
CASECONSTRAINT STATEclassification + dimensions + history
LIMIT / CONFLICTEvidence that weakens, reverses, narrows, or contradicts the claim.competing source · freshness · reversal · overlap
RULE

No silent deletion. No raw-volume winner. No “high-quality source = truth” shortcut. The downstream consumer gets an adjudicable evidence state.
