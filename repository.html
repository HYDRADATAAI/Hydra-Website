WHAT IT DOES

Market evidence becomes governed, traceable objects.

HYDRA is structured around ingestion, normalization, identity resolution, explicit handoff contracts, provenance/history, and auditable downstream interpretation.

DATA FLOW
SOURCE EVIDENCE ↓ NORMALIZATION ↓ IDENTITY RESOLUTION ↓ CONTRACT / AUTHORITY GATE ↓ PROVENANCE + HISTORY ↓ CONSTRAINT INTERPRETATION ↓ AUDITABLE RESULT
ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

Correctness is part of the pipeline.

Explicit authority, deterministic identity, provenance, contract-bound handoffs, testable transformations, preserved lineage, and blocked rather than fabricated outputs.

SHADOW CONTROL PRINCIPLE

Discoverable data is not automatically authorized data.

HYDRA treats field ownership as a contract question. A plausible value elsewhere in the project does not become admissible merely because it can be found.

ADMITrequired field + proven owner or permitted derivation
BLOCKmissing authority → stop → invent nothing
