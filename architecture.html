HYDRA / DATA · ARCHITECTURE

Make every handoff explain itself.

The architecture is organized around boundaries. A stage should know what it owns, what it accepts, what it emits, and what evidence must survive the transition.

CROSS-CUTTING SYSTEMS

The things that should survive every stage.

These are not decorative metadata. They are the connective tissue that lets an engineering team debug, reproduce, review, and trust the pipeline.

A

Lineage

Which input, stage, version, and transformation produced this output?

B

Provenance

Which source and authority justify the values and decisions represented here?

C

Determinism

Can the same sealed input reproduce the same artifact and receipt?

D

Quarantine

Can the system isolate suspect material without silently contaminating the next stage?

E

Mutation Audit

Can a closeout prove whether the frozen baseline changed during a repair?

F

Receipts

Can the test return evidence instead of requiring a reviewer to trust a narrative?

Visual meters are design emphasis, not maturity scores.
