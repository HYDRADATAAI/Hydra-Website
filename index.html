HYDRA / MARKET INTELLIGENCE & DATA ENGINEERING

Turn fragmented market data into traceable intelligence.

A data-engineering portfolio project built around controlled ingestion, explicit contracts, provenance, and auditable downstream decisions.

HYDRA ingests market, reference, and historical evidence, resolves identity, enforces contract-bound handoffs, preserves lineage, and produces downstream intelligence with an audit trail. Where authority is missing, the pipeline is designed to stop rather than manufacture a convenient answer.

SHADOW CONTROL PRINCIPLE

Discoverable data is not automatically authorized data.

Finding a plausible value somewhere in the project is not enough. The pipeline must prove that the source owns, or may legitimately derive, the semantic field required by the downstream contract.

AUTHORIZEDrequired field + valid owner/contract = admissible handoff
BLOCKEDdiscoverable data + missing ownership = stop, fabricate nothing
